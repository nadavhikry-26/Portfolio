Sticklight Cloud
hosting for vibe coding

Sticklight Cloud is a hosting platform built for the output of AI coding tools, a place for people to deploy, run, and manage the apps they build by vibe coding. I led it from an early idea to a working MVP, owning the product thinking, the UX, and the full interface.

Role

Product & Design (0→1)

Type

AI product

Scope

Product strategy, UX, UI, prototype

The problem

AI tools like Lovable, Cursor, and Bolt can generate a working app in seconds. But the moment you want to ship it properly, you hit a wall. Traditional platforms like Vercel and Netlify are built for developers who understand infrastructure, and the vibe-coding tools themselves are closed boxes you can't easily move out of. So a whole new kind of builder emerged: fluent in AI, but not in infrastructure, and stuck between tools that were too simple and tools that were too technical.

The solution

Sticklight Cloud closes that gap. It's hosting for AI-built apps that's powerful without being complicated. Three ideas hold it together:

  • Universal infrastructure. Professional-grade hosting built to support the output of any AI coding tool, with the stability and scale to run real products.
  • Magic wiring. Instead of asking users to configure infrastructure, the platform automatically detects the tech stack and connects the pieces behind the scenes.
  • Central hub. One place to deploy, monitor, and manage every AI app a user builds, across whichever tools they used to build them.

How I worked

This was an AI-native project in how it was built, not just what it does. I researched competitors, planned, and prototyped directly inside my own AI tooling, moving from idea to a working, clickable app fast. Sharing a live prototype let us catch edge cases, test the real UX, and return to Figma to finalize the design with confidence.
