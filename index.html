About

I'm Nadav, a senior product designer with more than 10 years of experience across a range of industries and both B2B and B2C products, working end to end: from research and product strategy through to the details of the final interface.

Lately my focus is on designing for the AI-native world: products where the hard problems aren't just visual, but about trust, control, and making powerful, unfamiliar technology feel obvious to use. I work AI-native myself, too: not only designing AI products, but building with AI tooling directly, from rapid prototyping to setting up my own workflows.

What I care about most is the hard part of the craft: taking something complex and ambiguous and making it feel simple, clear, and genuinely useful.
